# Avangard Global V2

Implement the consensus-approved plan in `.omx/plans/avangard-global-v2.md`.

The durable completion unit is the entire local V2 implementation: design contract and assets,
route/layout separation, additive migrations, versioned APIs, public site, Mini App, admin
improvements, tests, build, independent review, and adversarial QA. Production migration execution,
Vercel flag activation, and Gemini video delivery are excluded from local completion.
